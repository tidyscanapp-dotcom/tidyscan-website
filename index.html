How it works

  1. 1

    Scan

    Multi-page scanning with automatic edge detection and cropping. Straight and sharp, even on the kitchen table.

  2. 2

    Name

    TidyScan reads the first page and suggests date, sender and type: 20261112_Swisscom_Invoice instead of Scan_0047.

  3. 3

    File

    Smart folders pick the right destination, such as Finances, Taxes or Health. Confirm or change it with one tap.

Questions and answers

Which phones does TidyScan run on?

On Android 8.0 or newer with Google Play services. The scanner is downloaded once on first use.

Where are my PDFs saved?

In the folders you choose on your phone, for example Documents/Scans. TidyScan has no cloud of its own, so your files stay yours.

Does TidyScan work without internet?

Yes. Scanning and offline naming work without internet. Only the optional AI naming needs a connection.

What is sent with AI naming?

Only after you agree: a reduced image of the first page, the recognised text and your folder names. Only the suggestion comes back. Details in the privacy policy.

Which documents does it recognise?

Invoices, reminders, credit notes, quotes, receipts, contracts, policies, payslips, salary certificates, tax letters, statements, delivery notes and more, in eight languages. With AI naming, any document.
