Tax return documents: scan them once instead of searching every spring

Updated 18 November 2026 · Silvan, founder of TidyScan · 7 min read

A tax return rarely fails on the form. It fails on the pile that lands on the kitchen table in spring. This guide shows which tax return documents you need in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, how long to keep them, and how to file them digitally in seconds throughout the year instead of spending an evening searching in spring.

My spring ritual, until I'd had enough

For years I paid invoices straight away and then put the paper on a pile. I never filed it. Before the tax return I spent a whole evening sorting, punching holes and filing, and when I later needed a particular document I still searched for ages. TidyScan grew out of exactly that frustration.

The good news: the problem can almost entirely be moved to the moment a document arrives. Then it takes seconds instead of hours.

Which tax return documents you need

The forms have different names in every country, but the documents are surprisingly similar. These groups apply to almost every employee:

AreaTypical documents
IncomeAnnual salary statement: Lohnsteuerbescheinigung (DE), Lohnzettel (AT), Lohnausweis (CH); statements for pensions or benefits such as unemployment pay
WorkCommuting, further education, specialist books, work equipment, meals away from home: «Werbungskosten» in Germany and Austria, «Berufsauslagen» in Switzerland
Pensions and insuranceCertificates for private pension savings (pillar 3a in Switzerland), insurance contributions and premiums
HealthDoctor's, dentist's and pharmacy bills; usually only what you paid yourself counts
HomeService charge statement, invoices for tradespeople and household services; for owners, mortgage interest and maintenance
FamilyChildcare, education costs, maintenance payments
DonationsDonation receipts
AssetsBank statements as of year end, interest and dividend statements (in Switzerland for the securities list)

Not every line applies to you, and not everything counts the same way in every country. Which deductions exist is in the instructions for your tax return; in Switzerland this is the «Wegleitung» from your canton.

What doesn't belong in the tax folder

Not every slip of paper is a tax document. You can usually skip these three:

  • Everyday purchases unrelated to work: groceries, clothes, furniture. The exception is work equipment you really need for your job.
  • Duplicates: if there is an annual statement, you usually don't need the individual monthly invoices as well.
  • Medical costs your insurer covered in full: you can only deduct what you paid yourself.

Warranty receipts for devices don't belong in the tax folder, but they are still worth keeping, for example in a separate «Warranty» folder.

Scan receipts for your tax return: four steps, a few seconds

  1. Scan: document on the table, phone above it. TidyScan finds the edges and crops; several pages become one PDF. Invoices from emails you share straight from the mail app to TidyScan.
  2. Check the date: in the settings you can have TidyScan use the date on the document instead of the scan date. For tax documents that matters: the December invoice belongs to the old year, even if you scan it in January.
  3. Check sender and type: the text produces a name such as 20260214_Example-Ltd_Invoice.pdf. If something is wrong, change it in the dialog; the app remembers the correction for next time.
  4. File: the document lands in the folder you set up for taxes. For Swiss QR bills the amount goes into the name automatically.

A folder that still works in spring

Tax documents don't need a deep folder structure. One «Taxes» folder is enough, as long as the file name is right:

  • Date first: 20260214_… sorts chronologically and keeps a year together.
  • Sender in the middle: …_Example-Ltd_… finds everything from the same place.
  • Type at the end: …_Invoice.pdf tells you what's inside without opening the file.

So that documents land there automatically, give the folder keywords in TidyScan, such as «salary, donation, health insurance, tax». If one of them appears in the sender, the type or the text, the app picks that folder. How many folders you can create depends on your plan; the free plan has one. That works too, because the date comes first. More on folders and names is in the guides folder structure template and file naming convention.

In spring you then search your file manager or cloud storage for «2026», and every document of the year is together.

Is the scan enough, or do I need the original?

Day to day you work with the scan: it is legible, complete and found in seconds, three things a shoebox can't offer. Whether the authorities want to see the paper in a particular case isn't regulated the same way everywhere, though. In practice:

  • The scan must show the whole page. A cut-off amount helps nobody.
  • The paper originals of the current year go unsorted into a box until the tax assessment arrives. You no longer have to sort them; you have the scan for that.
  • Contracts, certificates and anything explicitly required in the original stay on paper.

Frequent questions

Which tax return documents do I need?

Everything that proves income or a deduction: your annual salary statement (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung, Lohnzettel or Lohnausweis), work expenses, pension savings, medical costs, childcare and donations, plus bank statements as of year end. What exactly is deductible is in the guidance of your country or canton.

Do I have to send the documents with my tax return?

In Germany and Austria, no: only if the tax office asks for them. In Switzerland you enclose the salary statement; which other documents are needed is up to your canton.

How long do I have to keep tax documents?

Private individuals in Germany and Switzerland have no general duty. In Germany keep them at least until the assessment is final (the VLH recommends four years). In Austria the Ministry of Finance asks for at least seven years. In Switzerland ten years make sense, because back-tax proceedings are possible for that long. Stricter rules apply to the self-employed.

Is a scan enough, or do I need the original?

For working with them, a complete and legible scan is enough. Keep the paper originals until the tax assessment has arrived; contracts, certificates and anything explicitly required in the original, for good.

Does TidyScan sort documents by year?

Every file name starts with the date, optionally the date on the document. So the documents of one year stay together, even if you only have a single «Taxes» folder.
